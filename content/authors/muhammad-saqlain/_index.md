Muhammad Saqlain is an MS student at the University of Education, Lahore. He has been selected for Google Summer of Code 2026 to work on [Recovering Historical Image Data Using Automated OrthoRegistration and Image Matching](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/programs/2026/projects/jLoU4DCV) with the DeepForest project. His work focuses on building an automated pipeline to align historical forest health survey annotations with satellite imagery, enabling large-scale training data recovery for ecological computer vision models.
